Experimental nuclear physics at the limit of the chart of the nuclides.
I work on in-beam and decay spectroscopy of heavy and superheavy nuclei
produced in fusion-evaporation reactions at the 88-Inch Cyclotron (LBNL) and
the K-130 cyclotron (JYFL). The work combines γ-ray, conversion-electron,
and recoil-decay-tagging techniques to characterize the structure of nuclei
approaching the predicted island of stability — including the recent Livermorium
production study and the development of focal-plane detectors for the
Berkeley Gas-filled Separator.
