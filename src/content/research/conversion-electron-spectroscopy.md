Conversion electrons carry the multipolarity information of a transition that
γ-rays alone cannot, but the experimental spectra are notoriously difficult
to interpret. I develop analysis tooling and apply combined
electron/γ-ray coincidence techniques to disentangle the decay schemes of
heavy and superheavy nuclei. The PhD work applied this to $^{250}$Fm at the
SAGE spectrometer (JYFL), using the K-isomer to test the predictions of
macroscopic-microscopic models.
