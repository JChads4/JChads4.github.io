Coupled-band structure in the transfermium region, where prolate and
triaxial shapes compete and K-isomers shape the observed decay patterns.
The PATRON code was developed specifically to simulate these strongly coupled
rotational cascades with side-feeding and detector response, and to overlay
the resulting spectra onto real experimental data from JYFL.
