Combining a semiclassical Superfluid Tunneling Model (Clark & Rudolph PRC 107,
034321) with a planned Physics-Informed Neural Network to predict
α-decay half-lives and hindrance factors from nuclear structure data. The
macroscopic tunnelling amplitude spans ~20 orders of magnitude; the
microscopic correction (~3–4 orders) is what the PINN is meant to learn.
The project uses the ENSDF-derived α-decay dataset and the published
Möller mass-table prescriptions as training inputs.
