I led the construction and commissioning of a position-sensitive avalanche
counter (PPAC) for the focal plane of the Berkeley Gas-filled Separator,
working with the Heavy Element Group at LBNL. The PPAC provides recoil-position
and time-of-flight information that is now being used to discriminate reaction
products in the superheavy element program and in the SHREC analysis pipeline.
A publication describing the construction and first on-beam results is in
preparation.
